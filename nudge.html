// MOBILE APP · FINTECH · AI ASSISTANT

Nudge: your money, explained

A personal finance app with two sides: a Home where you see your money at a glance, and an AI assistant you talk to when you want to understand it. The assistant doesn't wait to be asked, and it answers with charts you can touch instead of numbers buried in a sentence.

// 01 — THE PROBLEM

"I know I overspend a little. I just don't know where."

Most money apps are very good at showing numbers and very bad at explaining them. You see ₹41,200 spent, but not why, not whether it matters, and not what to do about it. Nudge was designed around one person and four honest assumptions.

WHO IT'S FOR

Arsh, 28 · Bengaluru

Salaried, about ₹85k a month. Pays for chai, cabs and late-night food with UPI. Saving for a Goa trip in December and a wedding next year.

// 02 — WHAT IT HAD TO DO

Three tests for every decision

01

Two sides, one loop

Home is where you see. Ask AI is where you understand. Every Home card is a door into the chat, and every chat ends with something that lands back on Home.

02

An agent, not a chatbot

The AI speaks first. It notices a pattern, asks a question back, and suggests an action. It never waits on a blank text box.

03

Show, don't tell

Every answer is a chart that points at the insight: highlighted, annotated and touchable, not a number buried in a sentence.

1

2

3

// 03 — HOME

Money weather: one glance, one feeling

The calm default puts things in order: what you can spend today, what's about to leave, and what changed this month. On top of it, the header turns spending pace into weather you feel instantly. Same layout, different moods, and the state is never colour alone: it's always an icon, a word and a reason.

TRY THE WEATHER

Every state comes with a next step

  1. Sunny · Keep Up. You're under pace. The header simply tells you to keep going.
  2. Cloudy · See Why. Something is drifting. The header becomes a door into Ask AI to find out what.
  3. Rainy · Let's Fix It. You're over pace. The header offers a fix rather than a warning.
  4. Safe to spend, explained. Limit = this month's income, minus bills and goal savings, spread over the days left. The number always carries its why.
  5. A question back, not a verdict. Shopping is up 64%. Is something coming up? One tap on A wedding or Festive season tags those spends and makes the next insight sharper.

Three questions Home could answer

I built the full Home three ways, each around a different question, to see which one earns the first screen.

Daily check-in

Today first

Safe to spend, bills due and one insight that asks back. It changes every day, so it earns a daily open.

Goal first

Great for savers

Progress, pace and a streak to celebrate. But for anyone without a goal yet, the first screen is empty.

Big picture

Better elsewhere

Net worth, cash flow and categories only move monthly. They work better in Accounts or as Ask AI answers.

A card library, every one a door into the chat

Each Home card answers one small question with a visual that points at the insight, and leads into Ask AI for the full story.

// 04 — ASK AI

Opening the chat without a blank box

The chat never starts empty. It can open with something it noticed, with questions picked from your data, or with a map of what you can explore, and voice is always one tap away, in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

// 05 — WHAT I TOOK AWAY

The chart is the answer

01

Design the loop, not two screens

Home raises the question and Ask AI answers it. Ending every chat in an action that lands back on Home is what makes the two sides feel like one product.

02

One template scales

Headline, visual, interaction, next step. Eight very different questions, and every answer still feels familiar.

03

Calm builds trust

Hidden numbers, no shaming and a tone the user chooses. Money is personal, so the product should never make someone feel judged.
