// 03 — ABOUT

I spent 3.5 years debugging production systems. Now I design the tools that make it less painful.

I started as a software engineer at Intel, shipping features for FlexRAN — virtualized 5G radio software — and living in the deploy → monitor → troubleshoot loop. Somewhere between reading logs at 2am and explaining dashboards to teammates, I realised the hardest problems weren't in the code. They were in how people see the system.

So I moved into design. Today I'm the first design hire at CodeXray, where I design with engineers rather than for them — turning telemetry, traces and AI investigations into interfaces an on-call SRE can trust.

Before all of that, I did my M.Tech at IIT Guwahati, where I co-authored a hardware-security paper — Corruption Exposes You, ISQED 2022 ↗.
