Product design assignment · AI personal finance

Paisa: an advisor that notices before you ask

A mobile finance app with two sides: one where you see and manage your money at a glance, and one where an AI agent helps you dig into spending, savings and goals. Every answer comes with a chart you can touch.

Role
Product designer (ex-engineer)
Market
India: Account Aggregator, UPI, ₹
Persona
Riya, 26, salaried, saving for Goa
Deliverables
Home screen, conversational AI

01 · Storyline

A Thursday night, 9:48pm

21:48 · THU 22 OCTRiya is about to order dinner again. She opens Paisa first, because the weather on Home has said "partly cloudy" all week.

HOMEShe has ₹460 left to spend today. An insight card from tonight's sync says food delivery is up 42%, mostly after 10pm on weeknights. She taps "Show me why".

ASK PAISAA treemap shows the orange food block is the biggest. Her orders aren't bigger, there are just more of them. A heatmap lights up Monday to Thursday after 10pm. A slider shows that cutting those orders by 70% gets her to Goa on time.

ACTIONShe sets a 10pm weeknight nudge. Paisa adds it to "What Paisa knows about you". She typed nothing; every step was one tap.

The whole design works backwards from this moment: the user finds the insight themselves, the AI guides each step, and the conversation ends with an action instead of a wall of text.

What I decided before designing

Each assumption closes off an ambiguity. The third column says what would change if the assumption turned out to be wrong.

03 · Constraints

Rules the design must follow

  • No fake real-time. Bank data refreshes up to 4 times a day. Today's spend comes from SMS and is labelled "estimated".
  • No made-up numbers. Every AI claim is calculated from the user's own data and shows its source ("Based on 26 Swiggy and Zomato orders").
  • Voice and chat give the same answer. Voice reads a short summary aloud and the chart stays on screen.
  • Choosing over typing. Onboarding and follow-ups use chips. Free text is supported and routed to the closest scenario.
  • Mobile, one hand. Charts are readable at 360px wide, with tap targets of at least 40px.
  • Privacy is visible. Consent can be revoked, and memory can be viewed and edited.

05 · Navigation

Three tabs, plus "Ask" on every widget

Home · Ask Paisa · Goals. The tab bar makes the brief's two sides obvious. The detail that makes it an agent is that every Home widget has an Ask button, which opens the conversation with that widget's context already loaded. The agent also sends work back: one insight card per sync on Home.

Alternatives I would defend in the interview

OptionGood forWhy not primary
Floating orb with a full-screen sheetAI feels present everywhere, Home keeps full widthHides the AI as a secondary feature, and the conversation history has no home

06 · Onboarding

Intent, consent, wait, aha

  1. Intent chips
    "What do you want your money to do?" Choosing "Save for a trip" opens sub-chips for place, amount and date, so the goal exists from minute one.
  2. Consent
    Three promises up front: read-only, a 12-month duration, revoke any time. Declining leads to sample-data mode, not a dead end.
  3. Designed wait
    Real steps and early findings ("found ₹85,000 on the 1st") while 6 months of data load. If it's slow, the user can leave and gets a notification.
  4. First insight and salary check
    One insight before the dashboard. The salary confirmation anchors every later calculation.
07 · Home

Understandable in 5 seconds, explorable for 5 minutes

Ordered by the question each widget answers, from "am I okay?" down to "what's next?".

WidgetQuestion it answersAlternative

08 · Ask Paisa

An exploration you steer, not a text dump

Every answer has the same structure, so people learn to scan it:

One or two sentencesInteractive chartSource and freshnessFollow-up chips

Five scenarios built

QuestionChartInteraction
Where's my money going?Treemap, food highlightedTap a block to see merchants
Why is this month higher?Waterfall, Sep → OctEach bar is one cause
Can I afford Goa in Dec?Projection lineDrag the slider and watch the date move
Any subscriptions I forgot?Recurring list with flagsSet a reminder before renewal
What are my habits?Weekday × time heatmapTap a cell for detail

Tone modes, with a guardrail

Neutral, Hype, Roast and Meme change the voice, never the numbers. Topics about debt or stress automatically switch to neutral and say so. Teasing someone who is worried about a bill would break trust.

Voice

Hold the mic and see a live transcript. The answer is read aloud as a short summary while the chart renders. Voice and text are two ways into the same answer.

09 · Personalisation

Driven by data, not by message count

My first idea was "personalise after about 100 messages". I replaced it because the data already exists on day one. Personalisation builds up in layers, and all of it shows in an editable memory panel.

LEVEL 1 · DAY 0Transactions: salary, bills, your normal, habits
LEVEL 2 · WEEKSCorrections: re-categorised payments, dismissed insights
LEVEL 3 · ONGOINGThings you say: goals, triggers, nudges you set

"What Paisa knows about you" lists each memory with its source. Forgetting one changes behaviour immediately.

The flows people usually miss

CaseHandling
One bank fails to syncRed stamp naming the bank and the age of its data, a banner with Retry. Numbers stay visible.
Salary not detected (freelancer, new job)Safe-to-spend asks for an income range with chips. Irregular income uses a 3-month average.
Messy UPI merchant"Help me sort" card with category chips. The answer applies to all similar payments.
Consent declinedSample-data mode with a persistent "Connect bank" banner.
SMS and bank record disagreeThe bank record wins after sync. Today's figure is always labelled "estimated".
Insight not useful"Not useful" lowers the ranking for that type of insight. The next card comes after the next sync.
Stressful topic in Roast modeSwitches to neutral automatically, explains why, and restores the mode on the next topic.
Question outside the five scenariosRouted to the closest scenario by keyword. In production: a general answer engine with the same structure.
Joint or shared accountsOut of scope for v1. Would need per-account inclusion toggles.
11 · What's next

What the agent could do beyond the five scenarios

  • Pre-emptive alerts: "Your card bill plus rent lands in the same week as a low balance."
  • Bill negotiation and switching: find a cheaper plan for a recurring payment.
  • Tax season: find 80C and 80D-eligible spending from transactions.
  • Shared goals: a Goa trip pooled with friends, with settlement.
  • Weekly recap: a 30-second voice summary every Sunday.

How I'd measure it

  • Insight usefulness: share of insight cards opened versus dismissed.
  • Asks from widgets as a share of all asks, which shows whether the agent pattern works.
  • Goals on track after 60 days.
